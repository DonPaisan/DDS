What we collect

When you use our site we collect the answers you give in our questionnaire, your name, phone number and email if you provide them, and technical information such as your device type, browser, approximate location, pages viewed, and how you interact with the questionnaire. We also collect advertising identifiers (for example the Meta click ID) so we can measure which ads bring people to our site.

How we use it

To contact you about debt relief options you asked about, to match you with a program, to improve this site, and to measure advertising. Sharing

We may share your information with debt settlement providers we refer you to, with service providers that run our website and communications, and with advertising platforms (such as Meta) in hashed form for measurement. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and tracking

We use first-party cookies and the Meta Pixel to understand site usage and ad performance. You can control cookies in your browser settings.
