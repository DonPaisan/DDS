    Common questions

    Asked on most calls.

    Is this a loan?

    No. We are not a lender. Debt settlement works by negotiating with your creditors to accept less than the full balance owed.

    Does the review cost anything?

    No. The review is free. A fee applies only if someone enrolls in a program and a debt is actually settled.

    What kinds of debt can be included?

    Unsecured debt such as credit cards, medical bills, personal loans, and accounts in collections. Mortgages, auto loans, and federal student loans generally cannot be included.

    Does debt settlement affect credit?

    It can, and the review says so plainly. Enrolling in a program may lower credit scores while accounts are being negotiated. It is not the right fit for everyone, and the review is where that gets worked out.

    Is my information safe?

    Yes. Your answers are private, used only to match you with the right option, and never sold. See our Privacy Policy.
